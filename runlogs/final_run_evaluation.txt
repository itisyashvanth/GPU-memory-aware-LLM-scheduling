Interpretation: lower thresholds (more conservative) drive OOM to zero but may leave some throughput on the table. Higher thresholds risk OOM creeping back up. The 'knee' of the OOM curve is the defensible choice for safe_threshold -- pick the lowest threshold that still achieves 0% OOM, rather than an arbitrary round number.
